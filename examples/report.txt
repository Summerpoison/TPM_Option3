LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Selection bias. Humans review candidates who reach the review step. Anyone
  rejected earlier is rarely looked at, so the override rate understates false
  rejections. Read it as agreement on the reviewed slice, not as accuracy.
* Small cells. Anything below n=10 is marked low confidence. Deep
  pipeline steps are small by nature -- that is what a funnel does.
* Reason matching is at concept level. A rejection citing a different specific
  requirement inside a category the job does use will not be flagged.
* Overrides inferred from an independent human decision are less certain than
  explicit rejections of the AI's suggestion; the counts are shown separately.
